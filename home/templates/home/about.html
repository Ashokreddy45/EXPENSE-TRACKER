About the project

Meet FinTrack

A personal finance management application designed to help users organise their financial records, understand their spending, plan budgets, and keep their financial goals in view.

Why FinTrack?

Managing money becomes difficult when expenses, income, budgets, and savings targets are scattered across different records. FinTrack brings these everyday financial tasks together in one application.

The problem it addresses

Without an organised record, it can be hard to remember where money was spent, compare spending with a budget, or see whether financial habits are changing over time.

FinTrack provides a structured place to record and review financial activity, helping users make sense of the information they enter.

What it aims to make easier

  • Maintain organised records of income and expenses.
  • Group transactions into relevant categories.
  • Set budget limits and savings goals.
  • Review available summaries and financial activity.

What you can do with FinTrack

The application brings together practical tools for everyday personal finance management.

Track expenses and income

Record money going out and coming in, with dates, amounts, descriptions, and categories where applicable.

Plan budgets

Create budget limits and use your recorded transactions to better understand spending against your plans.

Set financial goals

Define savings targets and deadlines to give your financial plans a clear objective.

Review financial activity

Use the available transaction views and summaries to identify patterns and make more informed everyday decisions.

Estimate income tax

Use the application's tax estimation feature to explore an estimate based on the details entered. Results should be independently verified.

Keep records organised

Use the application's available management features to maintain and update your personal finance records.

Who can benefit?

FinTrack is intended for people who want a clearer and more consistent way to manage everyday financial information.

  • Students: keep track of everyday spending and plan a limited budget.
  • Working professionals: organise income, recurring expenses, and savings targets.
  • Households: maintain a clearer record of spending and planned budgets.
  • Anyone building better habits: review financial activity and make more deliberate spending decisions.
A note about financial decisions: FinTrack helps organise the information users provide. It does not guarantee savings or financial outcomes. Tax estimates are informational and should be checked against current official guidance or a qualified tax professional.

Start organising your finances

Record your transactions, create a budget, or set a goal. Small, consistent steps can make your financial activity easier to understand.
